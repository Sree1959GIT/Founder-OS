# Northwind Analytics

Northwind Analytics is a fictional B2B SaaS company that sells a self-serve analytics workspace to mid-market
operations teams. Founded in 2023, about 25 staff, sold in USD on monthly and annual plans.

## Business model
- Three plans: Starter, Growth and Enterprise, billed per workspace.
- Acquisition channels: paid search, partnerships and outbound sales.
- See [[pricing-strategy|our pricing notes]] and the [[board-update-2026-q3|Q3 board update]].

## Goals for the next 12 months
1. Extend runway past 12 months without new financing.
2. Bring logo churn under 1.5 % a month (see [[customer-churn]]).
3. Decide whether to open a second sales region.
