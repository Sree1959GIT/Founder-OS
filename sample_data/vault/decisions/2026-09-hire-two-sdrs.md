# Decision: hire two SDRs for outbound

## Context
Outbound produced the best-converting pipeline last quarter, but one rep could not keep up with follow-ups.
[[Northwind]] has roughly ten months of runway, so every hire must pay back quickly.

## Options considered
1. Hire two sales development reps (SDRs) now.
2. Pilot first: one contractor SDR for 60 days, then decide.
3. Spend the budget on paid search instead.

## Decision
Hire two SDRs, with a kill criterion: if qualified meetings per SDR stay under eight a month by December,
pause the second seat. See [[pricing-strategy#Annual plans]] for the cash-flow lever we may pair with this.
