# Pricing strategy

Working notes on how [[Northwind]] prices its plans. Numbers here are narrative; the financial engine is the
source of truth for MRR and margins.

## Current plans
Starter is aimed at teams of up to five analysts. Growth adds scheduled reports and SSO. Enterprise adds audit
logs, a dedicated success manager and a custom data-retention policy.

## Price increase test
In November 2025 we raised list prices for new customers. Win rates in the pipeline held steady, which suggests
we were under-priced in the Growth tier. Existing customers kept their old price for twelve months.

## Annual plans
Offering a 15 % discount for annual prepayment would pull cash forward and lengthen runway. The risk is a lower
effective price per seat. Next step: model it in the scenario simulator before deciding.
