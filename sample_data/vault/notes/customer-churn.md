# Customer churn review

## Churn reasons
Exit interviews from the last two quarters point to three causes:
- Onboarding took too long for small teams, who left before seeing value.
- A competitor bundled dashboards with their data warehouse at no extra cost.
- Budget cuts at logistics customers after the freight slowdown.

## What we are trying
A guided onboarding checklist and a 30-day success call for every new Growth customer. The owner is the
customer success lead; results are reviewed monthly in the [[journal/2026-10-28|journal]].
