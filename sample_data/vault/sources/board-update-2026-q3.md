# Board update, Q3 2026

## Highlights
- Revenue grew quarter on quarter, led by the Growth plan.
- Two new SDRs approved ([[2026-09-hire-two-sdrs]]).
- Gross margin stayed above 70 %.

## Risks
- Runway is about ten months at the current burn.
- Logo churn among logistics customers is rising.
- A competitor bundles dashboards with its data warehouse.

## Asks of the board
Introductions to two mid-market logistics companies, and feedback on the annual prepayment idea.
